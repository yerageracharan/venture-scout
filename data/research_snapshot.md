## Research caveats

- No public dataset captures every private financing event.
- News search is discovery, not an authoritative record.
- Funding headlines can be incomplete, rounded, ambiguous about stage, or stale.
- Investor name frequency does not establish investment quality or causality.
- The starter cohort is selected, small and likely biased toward announced deals.
- No outcome labels exist yet, so no retrospective success analysis is justified.
