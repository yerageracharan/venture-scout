# Independent seed diligence rubric

This is an original, transparent synthesis of general venture practices. It is not a16z's proprietary scoring system.

| Dimension | Weight | Test |
|---|---:|---|
| Market size and urgency | 20% | Spend pool, growth, urgency, buyer and budget clarity |
| Founder-market fit | 15% | Domain insight, relevant experience, access, execution edge |
| Traction quality | 15% | Paid usage, retention, expansion, references, repeat demand |
| Problem intensity | 10% | Frequency, severity, status quo cost, urgency |
| Product wedge and 10x value | 10% | Measurably better initial workflow |
| GTM repeatability | 10% | ICP, channel, sales cycle, onboarding, repeat acquisition |
| Defensibility | 10% | Data rights, workflow depth, network effects, switching cost, IP, regulation, distribution |
| Capital efficiency | 5% | Milestones per dollar, gross margin, compute/hardware and working capital |
| Financing context | 5% | Stage fit, amount, use of proceeds, investor fit; terms only if disclosed |

## Rating anchors
1 = weak or contradicted; 2 = largely assertion/weak proxies; 3 = credible early independent evidence; 4 = strong repeatable evidence; 5 = exceptional, corroborated evidence.

A rating counts only with a written evidence note. Unknown is unassessed, never automatically 0 or 1.

weighted_points = (rating / 5) × dimension_weight

evidence_coverage = sum(weights for assessed dimensions)

score = sum(weighted_points) / evidence_coverage × 100

## Dispositions
- INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE: coverage <60% or fewer than five dimensions assessed.
- DEEP DIVE: score >=80 and sufficient coverage.
- WATCH: score 65–79.9 and sufficient coverage.
- LOW PRIORITY: score <65 and sufficient coverage.

The score is a research queue priority, not success probability, fair value, expected return, or investment advice.

## Evidence quality ladder
1. Regulator filing, audited statement, inspected product/customer evidence.
2. Company/investor primary source for facts they directly control.
3. Reputable independent reporting with named sources.
4. Industry database, aggregator roundup, newsletter, hiring page.
5. Social post or search snippet.

Red flags: maximum offering mistaken for amount sold; HQ confused with founder/investor address; SAFE/debt/extension mislabeled seed; pilots presented as paying customers; waitlists presented as retention; AI wrapper with no workflow/data/distribution advantage; unsupported founder/customer claims.
