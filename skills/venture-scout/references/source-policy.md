# Source policy

For each material claim store: claim, source title, publisher, direct URL, publication date, event date, source type, evidence status, excerpt, and limitations.

Funding statuses:
- announced_verified: primary company/investor source supports amount and round classification.
- announced_unverified: secondary article/headline lead; primary source not checked.
- sec_filing_only: Form D or securities filing exists; seed classification not established.
- database_only: database record only; no primary announcement.
- user_entered: user supplied; not independently checked.

For strict San Francisco searches, use official company HQ evidence. A registered agent, coworking directory, investor address, founder residence, or remote employee does not establish HQ. Track confirmed/probable/ambiguous/unknown.

Record round/event date separately from article publication date. Preserve reported currency. For conversion, record FX source, date, pair, and calculation.

Primary sources: company newsroom, investor announcement, regulator filing, official founder statement, product/customer documentation. Secondary sources: reputable press, trade publication, database, newsletter, roundup, social post. Secondary coverage can surface a lead but should not by itself upgrade funding to announced_verified.

Keep conflicting claims and explain which source is stronger. Recheck current status; if no reliable current source exists, leave status unknown.
